<section class="intro section container" id="about">
  <div class="section-kicker">THE BIG PICTURE</div>
  <div class="intro-copy">
    <h2>Hard experimental problems. <span>More than one discipline.</span></h2>
    <p class="lead">
      My research connects precision particle physics with the accelerator
      systems, targets, and instrumentation that make demanding experiments
      possible. I'm particularly interested in muon physics, rare processes,
      and the capabilities needed to push experimental frontiers.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="research section container" id="research">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <div>
      <div class="section-kicker">CURRENT RESEARCH</div>
      <h2>From targets to measurements.</h2>
    </div>
    <p>Building and understanding the systems that turn intense beams into scientific opportunity.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-grid">
    <article class="research-card">
      <div class="card-number">01</div>
      <div class="card-image card-image-target" role="img" aria-label="Production target hardware"></div>
      <div class="card-body">
        <p class="card-tag">MU2E · HIGH-POWER TARGETRY</p>
        <h3>Targets under extreme conditions</h3>
        <p>
          I lead development of the Mu2e production target. Target performance
          brings together particle production, heat transfer, materials
          behavior, radiation damage, mechanical integrity, and maintainability.
        </p>
      </div>
    </article>

    <article class="research-card">
      <div class="card-number">02</div>
      <div class="card-image card-image-instrument" role="img" aria-label="Beam instrumentation and diagnostic equipment"></div>
      <div class="card-body">
        <p class="card-tag">DIAGNOSTICS · MEASUREMENT</p>
        <h3>Making the beam measurable</h3>
        <p>
          I work on instrumentation for target-health monitoring and beam
          diagnostics, including fast RF-based measurements of beam intensity
          and structure.
        </p>
      </div>
    </article>

    <article class="research-card">
      <div class="card-number">03</div>
      <div class="card-image card-image-detector" role="img" aria-label="Particle detector or scientific visualization"></div>
      <div class="card-body">
        <p class="card-tag">DETECTOR R&D · PRECISION PHYSICS</p>
        <h3>New ways to measure</h3>
        <p>
          My research has also included directional neutron detection, tracking
          detectors, quantum-dot scintillators, and precision measurements at
          Belle II, including studies of lepton-flavor universality.
        </p>
      </div>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="future-section section" id="future">
  <div class="container future-layout">
    <div class="future-heading">
      <div class="section-kicker">LOOKING AHEAD</div>
      <h2>Mu2e is a beginning, not a boundary.</h2>
      <p>
        The challenges of high-power targetry and intense muon beams extend
        beyond any single experiment. I'm interested in how today's work can
        inform the next generation of facilities—and in the new challenges
        those facilities will bring.
      </p>
    </div>

      <article class="future-item">
        <span class="future-index">B</span>
        <div>
          <h3>Neutrino facilities: LBNF / DUNE</h3>
          <p>
            High-power targetry presents important challenges for intense
            neutrino beams, with its own requirements for particle production,
            thermal management, radiation damage, and reliable operation.
          </p>
        </div>
      </article>
    <div class="future-list">
      <article class="future-item">
        <span class="future-index">A</span>
        <div>
          <h3>Mu2e-II and the Advanced Muon Facility</h3>
          <p>
            Exploring future opportunities for precision measurements and
            rare-process searches involving muons, and the accelerator and
            target systems needed to enable them.
          </p>
        </div>
      </article>
      <article class="future-item">
        <span class="future-index">C</span>
        <div>
          <h3>The muon collider</h3>
          <p>
            I serve as Target Group Leader for the US Muon Collider Collaboration,
            contributing to the broader effort to develop the target concepts
            and capabilities a future muon collider would require.
          </p>
          <a class="text-link" href="https://muoncollider.us/contact">USMCC organization ↗</a>
        </div>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>
